Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is the ceremony?

Saturday, May 29, 2027 at St. Mina Coptic Orthodox Church in Calgary, Alberta. The ceremony begins at 1:30 PM — please be seated by 1:15 PM so you don't miss the entrance.

How do I RSVP?

Head to our RSVP page, which takes you to our Zola page where you can reply for everyone in your party. Please respond by April 1, 2027 so we can finalize our numbers.

What should I wear?

Formal / semi-formal attire. As the ceremony is held in a church, we kindly ask that shoulders and knees be covered out of respect for the space.

How long is the ceremony?

A Coptic Orthodox wedding ceremony typically runs about an hour and a half. If you haven't been to one before, expect beautiful chanting, incense, and plenty of tradition — everything you need to know will be easy to follow along with.

Where do I park?

Parking is available on site at the church. Please give yourself a few extra minutes to park and be seated before 1:15 PM.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes — children are welcome at the ceremony. Please just note who is coming when you RSVP so we have an accurate count.

Can I take photos?

We have a photographer capturing the day, so we'd love for you to be fully present during the ceremony itself. Photos afterwards are very welcome — and we'd love to see them!

Is there anything happening before the wedding?

Yes! We're hosting a casual backyard get-together on Friday, May 22, 2027 at 6:00 PM in Calgary — drop in anytime. Details are in the Join Us Early section above.

I'm travelling in — where should I stay?

Calgary International Airport (YYC) is about 20 minutes from the city centre, and there are plenty of hotels downtown. May is a busy month in Calgary, so we recommend booking early.

What about gifts?

Your presence is the greatest gift. If you'd still like to give something, our registry is on our Zola page.

Who do I contact if I have a question?

Just reach out to either of us directly — we're happy to help with anything we haven't covered here.

Still have a question? Get in touch — we'd love to hear from you.
